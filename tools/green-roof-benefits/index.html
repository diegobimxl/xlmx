BIM / Specification Notes

  • Revit shared parameters: GROOF_TYPE, GROOF_DEPTH_IN, GROOF_AREA_SF, GROOF_R_VALUE, GROOF_SW_RETENTION_PCT
  • Structural loading: extensive 10–25 lb/SF (saturated); intensive up to 120 lb/SF — structural engineer confirmation required
  • ASTM E2399: max media density for extensive green roofs; ASTM E2397: determination of dead loads
  • FLL Guidelines (Germany): international green roof standard; references for growing media depth and plant selection
  • Green roof membrane: EPDM or TPO with root barrier; min 60-mil EPDM under intensive systems; ASTM D6366 root resistance test
  • CSI Spec Section 07 55 63 (Fluid-Applied Waterproofing for Vegetative Roofing), 32 93 00 (Plants)
  • LEED v4.1 credits: SS Credit Heat Island (Option 2), WE Prerequisite Outdoor Water Use, SS Credit Rainwater Management
