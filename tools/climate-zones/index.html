BIM Project Setup

Set the ASHRAE Climate Zone as a Revit project parameter at project inception: ASHRAE_Climate_Zone and ASHRAE_Climate_Zone_Moisture (A/B/C). This feeds into all energy calculation tools. For LEED, the climate zone determines baseline ASHRAE 90.1 U-values, SHGC limits, and LPD requirements. For Passive House PHIUS 2021, climate zone determines site-specific annual heating and cooling energy targets (kBtu/SF/yr). The ASHRAE climate zone for any US location can be looked up in ASHRAE 169-2021 Table 1.1 or the IECC county lookup tool at energycodes.gov.
