IRS Compliance — IRC §4942
Private foundations must distribute at least 5% of the fair market value of non-charitable-use assets each year as "qualifying distributions." Qualifying distributions include grants, reasonable administrative expenses, and program-related investments (PRIs). Failure to meet the minimum results in a 30% excise tax on the undistributed amount. Excess distributions from prior years can be carried forward one year to satisfy the current year minimum. Consult qualified tax counsel for your specific situation.
