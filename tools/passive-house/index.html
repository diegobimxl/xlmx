BIM / PHPP Integration Strategy

Passive House certification requires PHPP (Passive House Planning Package) energy balance spreadsheet, which takes geometry directly from design drawings. Revit models should be configured with: accurate floor areas (treated floor area per PHPP definition), window areas and orientations per facade, thermal bridge ψ-values from THERM 2D calculations at all junctions. Export Revit room/space gross and net areas for PHPP input. Thermal bridge-free factor (fRsi ≥ 0.80) must be verified at all connection details using THERM — BIM XL can provide a THERM analysis library for standard connection types. Airtightness ACH50 measured via blower door at 50 Pa pressure difference.
