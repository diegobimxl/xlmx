BIM / Specification Notes

  • Revit shared parameters: STACK_HEIGHT_FT, STACK_INLET_SF, STACK_OUTLET_SF, STACK_CFM, STACK_ACH for natural ventilation documentation
  • Stack effect strongest when ΔT ≥ 10°F; reliable passive ventilation typically requires ΔT ≥ 5°F minimum — model worst-case mild day
  • CIBSE AM10 (Natural Ventilation in Non-Domestic Buildings): buoyancy-driven single-sided and cross-flow design guidance
  • Solar chimney: south-facing glazed shaft absorbs solar radiation, increases chimney air temperature by 10-30°F above ambient — increases stack effect by factor of 2-4x
  • Neutral Pressure Plane (NPP): air enters below NPP, exits above. Inlet openings should be below NPP, outlets above. NPP shifts down with larger outlet area.
  • IBC Section 1203: natural ventilation area requirement — openable area ≥ 4% of floor area OR mechanical ventilation per IMC
  • Wind-assisted stack: wind creates negative pressure at outlet (Bernoulli) — cowl/terminal design critical; adds 30-60% to buoyancy-only CFM at 5 mph wind
  • CSI Spec Section 23 37 00 (Air Outlets and Inlets), 08 90 00 (Louvers and Vents) — specify Cd and free area for stack openings
