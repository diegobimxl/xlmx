COP vs. AFUE

Gas efficiency tops out at ~98% AFUE. Heat pumps move heat rather than create it — COP 3.0 means 300% effective efficiency. At COP 2.5 + $0.12/kWh vs gas at 95% AFUE + $1.20/therm, heat pump wins when gas > $0.60/therm.

Cold Climate Performance

Modern cold-climate ASHPs (Mitsubishi, Bosch, LG) maintain COP 2.0+ at 0°F. Performance drops below -13°F. Resistance backup covers extreme peaks. GSHP maintains stable COP 3.5-4.5 year-round regardless of outdoor temperature.

IRA Incentives (2025)

§48C: 30% ITC for commercial heat pump projects. §25C: Up to $2,000 residential HP credit. §45L: $2,500-5,000/unit for HP-equipped new construction. HEEHRA rebates: up to $8,000 for qualified HP installation.

BIM / MEP Coordination Note

Tag heat pump systems in Revit MEP with parameters: HP_HeatingCOP, HP_CoolingCOP, HP_BackupType, AFUE_Baseline. For LEED EAc1 (Energy Performance), heat pump COP values feed directly into eQUEST/EnergyPlus baseline comparison. Ensure electrical panel capacity for heat pump loads is coordinated — typical commercial ASHP requires 3-phase 480V service. Document refrigerant type (R-410A phase-out: R-32 or R-454B replacements) in BIM model for long-term maintenance planning.
