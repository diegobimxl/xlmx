Developer ROI Framework — HVAC Upgrade Scenarios

VRF vs. standard packaged DX: +$8-15/SF first cost, saves 20-35% energy, 5-8 yr payback. Ground Source HP vs. standard: +$25-40/SF, saves 40-60% energy, 10-15 yr payback (qualifies for §48 ITC 30% credit through 2032). BIM / Drafting Note

In Revit MEP: use HVAC Zones to aggregate spaces for each AHU/FCU served. Assign Cooling Load and Heating Load parameters from energy model output. VRF systems: model as split system families with outdoor unit and indoor unit types — use manufacturer-provided Revit families (Daikin, Mitsubishi, LG). For DOAS + chilled beams, model beam units as air terminals with supplemental heating/cooling parameters. Link IFC model to EnergyPlus for detailed EUI validation.

Code + Incentive Reference

ASHRAE 90.1-2022: minimum efficiency requirements by equipment type (Table 6.8). All heat pumps (air, water, ground source) qualify for IRA §48 Commercial Clean Energy Credit (30%). Ground source: additional §45 credit potential. §179D Energy Efficient Commercial Building Deduction: $0.50-$5.00/SF for HVAC that exceeds ASHRAE baseline 25-50%. California Title 24 / IECC 2021: heat pump water heaters mandatory in new residential (most states).

EUI impacts are representative ranges for commercial office in climate zone 4-5. Actual values depend on envelope, internal gains, occupancy, controls, and climate. COP values at rated conditions (AHRI 210/240, 340/360). Costs are rough order of magnitude for commercial projects; residential costs 20-30% higher per SF. Always validate with mechanical engineer and manufacturer quotes.
