Storm Sewer Pipe Sizing (Manning)

Rational Method: Q = C·i·A (peak cfs for areas ≤ 200 acres). Manning pipe capacity: Q = (1.49/n)·A·R^(2/3)·S^(1/2) for full-flow. Minimum velocity = 2.5 fps (self-cleaning). Maximum pipe sizes shown for standard diameters. Tc assumed = 10 min for small sites; adjust i accordingly.
