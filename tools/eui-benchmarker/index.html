Energy Use Intensity Benchmarker

Compare building EUI against CBECS 2018 national medians by building type. Includes carbon estimate.

EUI (Energy Use Intensity) = Total Site Energy (kBtu) ÷ Gross Floor Area (SF). CBECS 2018 national medians shown. 1 kWh = 3.412 kBtu. 1 therm = 100 kBtu. LEED O+M Platinum typically requires EUI <50% of baseline.
