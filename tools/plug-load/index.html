BIM / Specification Notes

  • Revit shared parameters: PLUGLOAD_EPD_WSF, PLUGLOAD_ANN_KWH, PLUGLOAD_EUI_CONTRIB for energy modeling handoff
  • ASHRAE 90.1-2022 §12 Receptacle Controls: occupancy-sensor controlled receptacles required in open offices, private offices, conference rooms ≥50 SF
  • ENERGY STAR Most Efficient 2024: computers, monitors, servers, refrigerators, dishwashers — typically 30-50% less than standard equipment
  • DOE CBECS 2018: plug and process loads represent 35-44% of total commercial building EUI — largest single load category after HVAC in offices
  • Smart PDU (Power Distribution Unit) + occupancy-sensing controls can reduce plug loads 20-30% vs uncontrolled
  • LEED EA Credit 2 (Optimize Energy Performance): plug load reduction strategies count toward energy improvement %
