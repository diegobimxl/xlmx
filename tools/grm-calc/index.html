GRM Calculator

Gross Rent Multiplier quick valuation from purchase price and annual rent.

GRM (Gross Rent Multiplier) = Price ÷ Annual Gross Rent. A lower GRM means cheaper relative to income. Typical residential range: 4–12×. Commercial varies widely. GRM ignores expenses — always follow up with a full NOI analysis.
