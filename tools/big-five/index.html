O — Openness to Experience 50 · Average

Curiosity, creativity, and openness to new ideas and experiences. High scorers are imaginative and adventurous; low scorers prefer routine and convention.

C — Conscientiousness 50 · Average

Organization, dependability, and self-discipline. High scorers are diligent and goal-oriented; low scorers are more flexible and spontaneous.

E — Extraversion 50 · Average

Sociability, assertiveness, and positive emotionality. High scorers thrive in social settings; low scorers (introverts) prefer solitude and deep focus.

A — Agreeableness 50 · Average

Compassion, cooperation, and trust. High scorers are warm and conflict-averse; low scorers are more competitive and challenging.

N — Neuroticism 50 · Average

Emotional instability, anxiety, and moodiness. High scorers experience strong negative emotions; low scorers are emotionally stable and resilient.
