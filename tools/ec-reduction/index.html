BIM / Specification Notes

  • Revit shared parameters: MAT_EC_KGCO2E, MAT_EC_PER_SF, MAT_EPD_LINK for EC3 cloud integration via Revit EC3 Plugin
  • ISO 14044 / EN 15978: lifecycle stages A1-A3 (product), A4-A5 (construction), B1-B7 (use), C1-C4 (end of life), D (beyond boundary)
  • Concrete: specify 30-50% fly ash or GGBS (slag) cement replacement — reduces A1-A3 carbon 30-50% with no structural compromise
  • Steel: specify minimum 90% recycled content EAF (electric arc furnace) steel — EC factor 0.5-0.9 tCO2e/ton vs 1.8-2.2 BF-BOF
  • Mass timber (CLT/NLT): biogenic carbon sequestration credited at -550 kgCO2e/m³ (EN 16485) — significant structural carbon benefit
  • EC3 Tool (Building Transparency): free cloud database of 240k EPDs; filters by product category, distance, and declared unit
  • LEED v4.1 MR Credit: Building Product Disclosure — EPDs for 20 different products = 1 pt; MR Optimization — 10 products with low-carbon = 1 pt
