Pitch factor: Actual area = Plan area × √(1 + (rise/12)²). A 6:12 pitch = 1.118× plan area. A 12:12 (45°) = 1.414×. Flat/low-slope roofs (<2:12) use plan area directly. Add 10% for valleys, hips, and waste.
