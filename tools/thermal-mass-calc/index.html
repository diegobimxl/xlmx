BIM / Thermal Mass Integration

Assign thermal mass parameters in Revit as shared parameters on floor/wall types: ThermalMass_Lag_Hours, ThermalMass_Decrement_Factor, ThermalMass_kJ_per_K. For ASHRAE 90.1 energy models in EnergyPlus, exposed thermal mass directly affects HVAC peak sizing — a high decrement factor reduces peak cooling load, allowing equipment downsizing. Document exposed thermal mass area and depth for energy modelers. For Passive House PHPP, dynamic thermal mass is accounted for via the PHPP internal heat capacity input (kJ/K per floor area).
