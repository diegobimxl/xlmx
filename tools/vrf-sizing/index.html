BIM / Specification Notes

  • Revit shared parameters: VRF_ZONE_COOLING_MBH, VRF_ZONE_HEATING_MBH, VRF_IU_CFM, VRF_PIPE_RUN_FT, VRF_OU_TONS
  • AHRI 1230: standard rating conditions — cooling at 95°F OAT, heating at 47°F OAT; derate per manufacturer for design conditions
  • Pipe length correction: most manufacturers allow up to 300 ft equivalent; capacity derates ~1% per 10 ft beyond 100 ft
  • Diversity factor 0.8-1.0 is typical for commercial office; hotel/multifamily can be 0.6-0.75 due to occupancy diversity
  • Heat recovery systems recover rejected heat from cooling zones to satisfy simultaneous heating — net system COP can exceed 5.0
  • ASHRAE 90.1-2022 minimum EER for VRF: EER ≥ 12.5 for split system >65 kBtu/hr cooling — verify equipment schedules
  • CSI Spec Section 23 81 26 (VRF Multi-Split Heat Pump) or 23 81 27 (Variable Refrigerant Flow HVAC Systems)
