Assign insulation properties in Revit material library: R_Value_per_Inch, GWP_kgCO2e_SF_per_inch, Vapor_Permeance_Perms, Fire_Rating. For LEED EQc2, low-emitting materials require VOC certification — spray polyurethane foam must have third-party emissions testing. For LEED MRc2 (Building LCA), embodied carbon of insulation is a material flow input to EC3 or Tally. Continuous insulation (CI) on metal-framed walls is accounted for separately from cavity insulation in ASHRAE 90.1 Table A3.3 adjustment factors. Document CI type and R-value per inch as a Revit wall layer parameter for automated U-value calculations.
