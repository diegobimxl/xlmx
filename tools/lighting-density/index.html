BIM / Revit Lighting Coordination

Assign ASHRAE 90.1 parameters in Revit lighting fixture families: LPD_Watts, LPD_Space_Limit_W_SF, LPD_Compliant. Schedule all lighting fixtures with calculated LPD per space. For LEED EAc1 (Energy Performance), lighting power density is a direct input to the energy model — export from Revit schedule to energy modeler. ASHRAE 90.1 §9 (Lighting) requires occupancy sensors in spaces >250 SF, daylight responsive controls in daylight zones, and auto-shutoff in spaces <5,000 SF. Tag non-compliant spaces in Revit with LPD_Flag = OVER_LIMIT for design review coordination.
