BIM / Specification Notes

  • Revit shared parameters: FACADE_SYSTEM, FACADE_UVAL, FACADE_SHGC, FACADE_VT, FACADE_COST_SF, FACADE_AREA_SF
  • NFRC 100: U-factor and SHGC whole-window ratings (not center-of-glass) — specify in window schedule with NFRC label
  • Curtain wall CSI Spec Section 08 44 13 (Glazed Aluminum Curtain Walls); BIPV Section 08 44 26 (Building Integrated Photovoltaic Panels)
  • CI (Continuous Insulation) eliminates thermal bridging through stud framing — required per ASHRAE 90.1-2022 in CZ 3+ for non-residential
  • ASHRAE 90.1-2022 Appendix A: assembly U-factors and C-factors for compliance path; alternatively use Table 5.5 for prescriptive envelope
  • Dynamic glazing (electrochromic): SHGC variable from 0.09 to 0.36 on demand — maximize daylight while controlling solar gain; premium $80-120/SF
  • BIPV note: PV-integrated spandrel panels generate 12-18W/SF peak DC; include electrical design scope for grid-tie inverter and code compliance
