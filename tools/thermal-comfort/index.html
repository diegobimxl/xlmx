Export operative temperature (avg of air + MRT) for EnergyPlus/IES-VE validation. LEED EQc5 (Thermal Comfort) requires ASHRAE 55 compliance documentation and post-occupancy survey for 90% occupied zones. Tag non-compliant spaces with ThermalComfort_Flag = YES for QC coordination.

Operative Temperature

Weighted average of air temperature and mean radiant temperature. For air speeds below 40 ft/min, operative temp ≈ (Ta + Tr) / 2. ASHRAE 55 adaptive comfort uses operative temperature as the primary variable.

Adaptive Comfort Model

For naturally ventilated spaces, ASHRAE 55 permits the adaptive model: comfort zone expands with outdoor temperature. Allows up to 80% acceptability at higher indoor temperatures — key for passive design strategies.
