Developer ROI Quick Guide

Double Low-E argon (#3 passive, U=0.28) vs. clear double: ~$8-12/SF premium → typical 4-7 yr payback in climate zone 5+. Triple glazing adds $20-30/SF vs. standard double but cuts infiltration losses 60%. Electrochromic: $100-180/SF premium but eliminates roller shades, reduces HVAC tonnage 8-12%, qualifies for §179D deduction.

BIM / Drafting Note

In Revit: use the Glass material parameter to assign U-value and SHGC to curtain wall panels. Map SHGC to the Solar Heat Gain Coefficient parameter; U-Value maps to Thermal Resistance (R = 1/U). For energy analysis in Insight or eQUEST, export SHGC per orientation as the system defines orientation-specific gains. In IES-VE, assign glazing construction directly from the manufacturer database (e.g., Guardian SunGuard, Vitro Solarban).

Code Reference

ASHRAE 90.1-2022: glazing U-value and SHGC limits by climate zone in Table 5.5-1 through 5.5-8. ENERGY STAR Residential: U ≤ 0.27 north, U ≤ 0.30 south. IECC 2021: prescriptive path Table R402.1.2. LEED v4 EQc8 (Quality Views): minimum VLT ≥ 0.27 for view glazing. Section 179D deduction: envelope must beat ASHRAE 90.1 baseline by 25%+ for full deduction.
