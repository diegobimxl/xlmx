UTM Zone Reference

UTM divides the world into 60 zones of 6° longitude each, numbered 1–60 west to east.
Mexico City ≈ Zone 14N | New York ≈ Zone 18N | London ≈ Zone 30U
Northing increases northward from equator (N hemisphere) or from 10,000,000m (S hemisphere).
