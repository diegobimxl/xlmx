Temperature-Entropy Diagram — Key Concepts
The Carnot cycle forms a perfect rectangle on a T-S diagram. Four reversible processes: isothermal expansion at T_H (heat absorbed), adiabatic expansion, isothermal compression at T_C (heat rejected), adiabatic compression. Area enclosed = net work output. No real engine can exceed this rectangle's efficiency for given T_H and T_C. The ratio T_C/T_H defines the irreducible fraction of energy that must be rejected — this is not a design flaw, it is a law.
