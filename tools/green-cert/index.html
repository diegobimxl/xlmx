LEED
v4.1 BD+C / ID+C / O+M
World's most widely used green building rating system. Points-based across 9 categories. Four certification levels: Certified (40+), Silver (50+), Gold (60+), Platinum (80+). Strong on energy, water, materials, and indoor environment. Market-recognized for commercial real estate premium.
WELL
v2 Q4 2023
Focuses exclusively on occupant health and wellness — air, water, nourishment, light, movement, thermal comfort, sound, materials, mind, community, innovation. Bronze (40+), Silver (50+), Gold (60+), Platinum (80+). Often pursued alongside LEED for premium office/residential.
PHI / PHIUS
PHIUS 2021 / PHI Classic
Strict energy performance standard with quantitative thresholds: max heating demand 4.75 kBtu/SF/yr (PHIUS), airtightness ACH50 ≤ 0.6. Not a points system — pass/fail. Highest energy performance standard available. Cost premium typically 5–15%. Best in cold/mixed climates.
BREEAM
2018 NC / In-Use
UK-origin, widely used in Europe, Middle East, and emerging markets. Categories: Management, Health/Wellbeing, Energy, Transport, Water, Materials, Waste, Land Use, Pollution. Rating: Pass (30%), Good (45%), Very Good (55%), Excellent (70%), Outstanding (85%). Strong on Materials and Transport criteria.

BIM / Documentation Strategy

For LEED, energy model in EnergyPlus/eQUEST must be coordinated with Revit model — use Autodesk Insight or IES-VE gbXML export. All MEP system parameters must match the BIM model. For WELL, coordinate with lighting designer in Revit for sDA/ASE analysis (Radiance-based). For Passive House, run PHPP energy balance in parallel with Revit geometry — use THERM for thermal bridge PSI-value calculations at connections. BIM XL recommends a unified Revit model with LOD 350 envelope assemblies and shared parameters mapped to all certification credit calculators.
