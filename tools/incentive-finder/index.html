BIM Documentation for Tax Credits

§179D requires a qualified energy study by a licensed engineer certifying 25%+ or 50%+ energy savings — this must be backed by an ASHRAE 90.1 Appendix G energy model from your BIM model (EnergyPlus or approved software). §45L requires ENERGY STAR or DOE Zero Energy Ready Home certification with HERS rating — coordinate with energy rater. §48 ITC requires IRS Form 3468 and documentation of qualifying property placed in service. BIM XL can provide the energy model and documentation package. All incentive claims should be reviewed with a tax professional — dollar amounts shown are estimates only.
