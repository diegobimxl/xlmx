Cultural Dimensions — Hofstede

Category: Anthropology — 6-dimension cultural comparison

Dimension——Delta
DIMENSION DESCRIPTIONS
PDI — Power Distance: Acceptance of unequal power distribution.
IDV — Individualism: Preference for individual vs collective action.
MAS — Masculinity: Competition, achievement vs care and cooperation.
UAI — Uncertainty Avoidance: Tolerance for ambiguity and the unknown.
LTO — Long-term Orientation: Future-oriented vs present/past focus.
IND — Indulgence: Gratification of desires vs strict social norms.
