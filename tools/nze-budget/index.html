Reduce Heating/Cooling

High-performance envelope (R-30+ walls, triple glazing), SHGC optimization, airtightness <0.4 ACH50. Each R-5 CI added to walls reduces heating EUI by ~1-2 kBtu/SF in CZ 4-6.

Reduce Lighting

LED + occupancy/daylight controls can reduce lighting EUI to 3-5 kBtu/SF/yr in offices. ASHRAE 90.1-2022 LPD for office: 0.64 W/SF. Daylight-responsive controls mandatory in perimeter zones.

Increase PV Supply

Ground mount, parking canopies, and facades can supplement rooftop PV. Bifacial panels add 10-20% yield. Battery storage (BESS) allows time-shifting and grid independence for LEED Zero.

Reduce Plug Loads

Plug loads are often 20-30% of office EUI. ENERGY STAR equipment, smart power strips, occupancy-controlled outlets, and tenant engagement programs can cut plug loads by 20-40%.

ERV / DOAS

Energy Recovery Ventilation captures 70-80% of exhaust energy. DOAS with ERV reduces HVAC EUI by 15-25% in commercial buildings. Critical path for NZE in humid climates.

LEED Zero Criteria

LEED Zero Energy: 12-month net zero site energy. Requires metering, on-site generation documentation, or verified RECs. No minimum energy performance threshold — combine with LEED Platinum for full package.

BIM Energy Model Integration

NZE requires ASHRAE 90.1 Appendix G whole-building energy model (EnergyPlus/eQUEST/IES-VE). Export Revit geometry via gbXML for energy model input. Assign all mechanical system parameters as Revit shared parameters for model accuracy. PV system output from PVWatts or SAM (NREL) must match the Revit roof geometry area and orientation. For LEED Zero, meters must be installed on all end-use categories — coordinate with MEP engineer for Revit MEP meter placement. Use Autodesk Insight for rapid EUI optimization during schematic design.
