BIM / Specification Notes

  • Revit shared parameters: EUI_BASELINE, EUI_TARGET, EUI_AFTER_ECM, ECM_CAPEX_TOTAL for energy model handoff to EnergyPlus/OpenStudio
  • ASHRAE 90.1 Appendix G: Performance Rating Method uses energy cost budget (ECB) — EUI reduction % maps to EA points in LEED v4.1
  • DOE CBECS 2018 median office EUI: 65 kBtu/SF/yr. ENERGY STAR Office score 75 ≈ 45 kBtu/SF/yr. Net Zero ≈ 25-35 kBtu/SF/yr
  • ASHRAE 50% Advanced Energy Design Guides (AEDG): free prescriptive pathways to 50% EUI reduction by building type
  • Passive House (PHIUS 2021): space conditioning ≤4.75 kBtu/SF/yr — requires comprehensive envelope + mechanical overhaul
  • IRA 2022 §179D: $2.50-5.00/SF deduction for commercial buildings achieving 25-50%+ energy cost reduction vs ASHRAE 90.1 baseline
